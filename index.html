Maintenance Note — V.A.C. Sequence

Phase 1a — light up 2 through 6, leave 1 off

  1. Click all six lights once each so they're all green.
  2. Click the fifth light — its green neighbor (the sixth) turns blue.
  3. Click the sixth, then fifth, then fourth, then third, then second, then first (each now blue in turn) — each click lights up both neighbors next to it (green ones and the off one left behind by the last click), while the light you clicked switches off itself. The "off" gap just walks down the line.
  4. After clicking the first light, lights two through six are all blue and light one is off.

Phase 1b — turn that blue into purple, one at a time

  1. Click the first (off) light twice — green, then red.
  2. Click the second (blue) light — it turns the first light purple and switches off.
  3. Click the second light twice — green, then red.
  4. Click the third (still blue) light — it turns the second light purple and switches off.
  5. Click the third light twice — green, then red.
  6. Click the fourth (still blue) light — it turns the third light purple and switches off.
  7. Click the fourth light twice — green, then red.
  8. Click the fifth (still blue) light — it turns the fourth light purple and switches off.
  9. Click the fifth light twice — green, then red.
  10. Click the sixth (still blue) light — it turns the fifth light purple and switches off.
  11. Click the sixth light twice — green, then red.
  12. Click the sixth light once more — with a purple neighbor, it turns purple too. All six are now purple.

A light only ever turns purple by having a blue light clicked next to it — an off light with a red neighbor just turns itself green. Blue always switches itself off the moment it's clicked, whether it's lighting up green neighbors or turning a red one purple.

Phase 2 — place white and settle

  1. Click the fourth (purple) light to turn it white.
  2. Click the sixth (purple) light to turn it blue.
  3. Click the sixth (blue) light — it turns off, and the blue moves to the fifth (taking over the purple there).
  4. Click the sixth light twice — green, then red.
  5. Click the fifth (blue) light — it turns off, and the sixth light turns purple.
  6. Click the fifth light twice — green, then red.
  7. Click the second (purple) light once — it turns blue.
  8. Click the third (purple) light three times — blue, then off, then green.
  9. Click the first (purple) light twice — blue, then off. Power restored.
The panel is only fully restored once the strip reads gray, blue, green, white, red, purple left to right. Every light starts off (gray) and just turns green when clicked — purple only ever comes from clicking a blue light next to a red one.
BREAKERS

Reset returns every light to off. Undo steps back one click.
